Implementation limits:
  - The discount flag uses existing OD corridor selection and captured running,
    passenger-capable aircraft. It is conservative about future connection times
    and seats, and retains potential flights outside the first horizon.
  - Once an optional wider search exceeds its existing allowances, the best
    validated cached journey is used; global optimality beyond those allowances
    is not claimed. No budget or maximum horizon is increased.
  - Dominance still requires the same vehicle/station path and all prior
    capacity/certainty conditions. A larger earlier aircraft adjustment cannot
    dominate a cheaper later prefix. Weighted waits include the extra wait to
    a common departure.
  - No packet-level network traversal, persistent table format, asynchronous
    publication rule, CargoDist calculation, or settings invalidation is added.
